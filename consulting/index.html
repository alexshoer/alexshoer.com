Services

Turning a promising market into a working business.

Spotting where a market is going is the easy part. I help teams with the hard part: the plan, the evidence, and the capital to get there.

01

Go-to-market plans

Who to sell to first, how to reach them, and what to say. Positioning, pricing logic, channel and partnership strategy, and a plan your team can run.

02

Market intelligence

Clear answers on market size, competitors, customers, and policy. Including how FEOC rules, tariffs, and the China supply chain affect your plans.

03

Fundraising support

Sharpening the story, the deck, and the model, preparing for investor questions, and thinking through which investors fit your stage and sector.

Other needs, like partnership strategy, market entry, or a second opinion before a big decision, are welcome too. Ask.

How it works

Two ways to work together.

AdvisoryOngoing support for founders and leadership teams: regular working sessions, a sounding board on strategy and fundraising, and introductions where they help.
ProjectsA defined piece of work with a clear scope, timeline, and deliverable, such as a go-to-market plan, a market study, or fundraising preparation.

It starts with a short call to understand what you need. If it’s a fit, I’ll follow up with a simple proposal covering scope, timing, and fees.
